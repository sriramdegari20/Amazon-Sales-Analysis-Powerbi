# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

A data analytics project focused on exploring Amazon product sales data, customer engagement, product categories, and market trends using Microsoft Power BI and Excel.

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales Analysis** project aims to transform raw data into meaningful business insights through interactive Power BI visualizations and data analysis.

This project explores product portfolio growth, customer reviews, category distribution, and shipping market information to understand business performance and identify potential opportunities.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze Amazon product data and performance.
- Understand product category distribution.
- Explore customer engagement through product reviews.
- Examine shipping market information.
- Identify business growth opportunities.
- Present key insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Product Growth:** The product portfolio contains more than 12,500 products, with reported year-over-year catalog growth of 12%.
- **Customer Engagement:** The presentation reports an average review metric of 25,847.
- **Category Diversification:** Products are distributed across more than six major categories.
- **Market Focus:** Bangladesh is identified as the primary shipping market.
- **Growth Opportunities:** Targeted marketing and expansion into similar emerging markets are highlighted as potential strategies.
